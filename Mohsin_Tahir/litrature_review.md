9/30/26(wednesday)
Read https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.25464
- Used optical spectra from SDSS
- Used Equivalent widths to identify spectrel types of O-type stars
- Rather than using He spectrel lines it uses Nitrogen lines to identify Hot O-type stars(O1-O3)
 ## pros
Provides the best alternative for classifying hot O-type stars, since He lines in these massive stars are heavily contaminated by strong stellar winds.
## cons
-Cannot physically identify the spectral type and luminosity class instead, it forces the use of empirical labels for classification.

read https://arxiv.org/pdf/1702.06957
- used optical spectra from sdss
- used template matching to classify all the stars from the MK system (we are going to be focusing on O-type stars)
- To ensure quality for template matching, the photometric errors had to be $e < 0.1$ magnitudes, and the r-band interstellar extinction was capped at $A_r < 0.25$ magnitudes to eliminate artificial reddening
## pros 
- the data is purely empirical data which yeilds realistic results 

## cons
- small sample size for O type stars
- it fails to distinguish between dwarf and giant luminosity classes for O and B types rather than then distinguishing it groups them together
