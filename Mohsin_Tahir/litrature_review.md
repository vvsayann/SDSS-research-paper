Read https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.25464
- Used optical spectra from SDSS
- Used Equivelent widths to identify spectrel types of O-type stars
- Rather then using He spectrel lines it uses Nitrogen lines to identify Hot O-type stars(O1-O3)
 ## pros
Provides the best alternative for classifying hot O-type stars, since He lines in these massive stars are heavily contaminated by strong stellar winds.
## cons
-Cannot physically identify the spectral type and luminosity class instead, it forces the use of empirical labels for classification.
